Petition Intake
Slide 01

Petition intake

A petition can start from any of these channels. The public uses the portal and QR form to create a petition and track their case.

IVR / Helpline

Call recordings from the helpline become a petition.

Social media listening

Complaints from official social accounts are captured.

OCR reading

Handwritten papers, scans and email attachments are read by OCR.

Email

The integrated mailbox turns incoming mail into a petition.

WhatsApp

Texts, images and voice notes are accepted as intake.

QR petition

The public scans a QR code to open the form and create a petition.

Public portal & tracking

Citizens submit on the portal and track their own case status.

Offline

Walk-in visits and physical mail are entered as a petition.

Every channel creates one common petition record. No separate file per channel.

Reference material

Design reference

Dark blue and cyan VCEMS kit. Five slides: Petition intake, FIR Generation & Management, Case Management System, Features of AI, and Case Diary. Small cards with one-line points.

Petition intake

Channels: IVR / helpline, social media listening, OCR reading, email, WhatsApp, QR petition form, public portal with case tracking, and offline walk-in or mail. All channels create one common petition record. The public can create a petition by QR or portal and track their case on the portal.

FIR Generation & Management

Petition data auto-fills FIR Information. Officers review with no re-entry. Completed FIR flows into the FIR Module. FIR activities include FIR Info, Acts, Occurrence, Complaints, FIR Content, Accused, Signature, Upload and Action.

Case Management System

VCEMS is integrated with FIR. Case Diary starts automatically. Templates, digital arrest, search, download, digital signatures, court submission and checklists support case work.

Features of AI

Summarizing, AI Minutes, field extraction, language and OCR, and department routing. Officers review every AI draft.

Case Diary

Single case record with unique document IDs, page count, date, digitized informational reports and traceability.
