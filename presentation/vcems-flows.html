VCEMS Enhancement

From the existing FIR platform to a digitized case, document and arrest workflow. Petition data auto-fills FIR. FIR auto-starts Case Diary. Documents, arrest, search and court submission stay digital.

EXISTING SYSTEM TODAY

FIR-centric, paper after registration

Master, Activities and Reports already support FIR documentation, users and units. After FIR registration, investigation work, memos, arrests and court submission remain largely manual.

WHAT THIS CHANGE ADDS

Automatic data flow into a digital case file

Petition data flows into FIR Information. Completed FIR opens Case Diary. Templates, unique document numbers, digital arrest, search, signatures and court submission complete the record.

VCEMS / Petition → FIR → Case Diary → court. No duplicate entry of petition data.

The Digital Gap

Despite this digital infrastructure, the investigation process — from entrustment to final report — is still handled manually. Documents are prepared and maintained in physical form, requiring manual compilation, approvals and communication between stakeholders.

This results in delays, added workload, and risk of losing critical case information.

Existing System

Existing System — Manual Investigation Process

FIR registration is digital. The investigation that follows is still a paper-based chain of documentation, approvals and physical court submission.

Today's paper-based flow
  1. 1

    FIR registered by SHO / authorized officer

  2. 2

    Case entrusted to an Investigating Officer (IO)

  3. 3

    Field evidence collection & interrogation

  4. 4

    Reports, memos & arrests (if any) documented

  5. 5

    Stage-wise paper approvals to supervisory officers

  6. 6

    Final Report prepared manually & submitted physically to court

Key challenges

  • Manual & paper-based

    Documents are prepared, compiled and approved physically at every stage.

  • No real-time tracking

    Difficult to monitor case progress or enforce timelines.

  • Fragmented approvals

    Separate paper sign-offs across officers, units and the Directorate.

  • Risk of data loss

    Physical records are prone to misplacement or damage.

  • Slower decision-making

    Increased administrative workload and turnaround time.

Case Diary

Document identity

Each document has a unique Document ID / Number. Every document and relevant item associated with the case is assigned a unique number for identification and tracking.

New feature

Activity-based checklist & progress monitoring

Each major activity contains a predefined set of sub-activities. Checklist completion is used to monitor activity progress and to generate management-level reports.

Checklist for every activity

A dedicated checklist is created for every activity. Officers can clearly identify the activities that must be completed before closing an activity.

  • Each major activity has a predefined set of sub-activities.
  • Each activity has a predefined checklist.
  • Checklist-based processing ensures required steps are not missed.

What officers and management can track

  • Activity status
  • Sub-activity completion
  • Pending activities
  • Completed activities
  • Overall case progress

Reports are generated from activity and checklist completion. This supports monitoring, review and management-level reporting, with better visibility into each case.

Existing system

VCEMS is an operational web platform for VACB. Scale used in this deck: 1,000+ registered users, 24 offices, ~500 FIRs per year. Core modules today: General Diary / FIR Registration, User Management, Unit Management, and stakeholder integration. FIR Activities include FIR Info, Acts, Occurrence, Complaints, FIR Content, Accused, Signature, Upload and Action. Investigation after FIR remains paper-based.

Proposed change

Petition data auto-fills FIR Information with no re-entry. Completed FIR auto-creates the first Case Diary section. Case Diary holds uniquely numbered documents. Informational reports are digitized. Authorized templates, digital arrest memo, arrest information (including medical reports), search, single/bulk download, digital signatures and digital court submission follow. Checklist completion drives progress monitoring and management reports. Aadhaar verification is subject to feasibility and authorization.
