🏗️ How the Platform Works

Simple step-by-step flows explaining what happens when a customer calls or chats with Sheetal AC.

How Inbound Phone Calls are Handled

From the moment the phone rings to the moment a technician is scheduled in Sheetal CRM:

Step 1
Customer Calls
A customer dials Sheetal AC's phone number. If a call drops or disconnects, the system automatically calls back within 45 seconds.
Step 2
Instant AI Greeting
The AI answers on the 1st ring: "Hello, welcome to Sheetal AC. How can I help you today?" (available in Malayalam or English).
Step 3
Understands Customer Need
Identifies whether the customer wants a new AC, needs technician service, has a complaint, or needs quick troubleshooting.
Step 4
Action & Confirmation
Collects location and preferred appointment slot, books the ticket in Sheetal CRM, and sends an SMS & WhatsApp confirmation.
Human Safety Backup: If a customer asks to speak with a person, or has a complex request outside standard workflows, the AI immediately transfers the call to Sheetal AC front-desk staff with a brief audio summary of what the customer said.

SRV Social WhatsApp Flow (Connected to Sheetal CRM)

Sheetal CRM already has its own SRV Social WhatsApp system. The AI assistant connects directly to this existing setup:

Step 1
Customer Messages
Customer sends a message to Sheetal AC's official WhatsApp number (e.g. "My AC is leaking water").
Step 2
SRV Social Ingestion
The message lands directly in Sheetal CRM's SRV Social module, which passes the text to our AI assistant in real time.
Step 3
Step-by-Step Chat
The AI asks 1 question at a time (AC brand, area, preferred date & time) and asks the customer to confirm with a simple "YES".
Step 4
CRM Sync & Staff View
The service ticket is created automatically in Sheetal CRM. Sheetal staff can view the full conversation inside the SRV Social dashboard anytime.
Live Staff Chat Takeover: Because this uses your existing SRV Social WhatsApp in Sheetal CRM, your staff can intervene and reply to any customer directly from the CRM chat panel at any moment. The AI automatically pauses once staff take over!

Automatic Record Creation in Sheetal CRM

Call Audit

🛡️ Safety Guardrails & Business Rules

Built-in safeguards ensure professional customer handling, compliance, and zero dropped calls.

📞 Zero Dropped Call Guarantee
If a customer's phone signal is weak or the AI encounters any delay, the call is transferred smoothly to Sheetal AC front desk with an audio whisper summary.
⏰ TRAI Business Hours Compliance
Automated outbound callbacks strictly follow official calling hours (9:00 AM – 8:30 PM). Night-time missed calls receive an instant WhatsApp acknowledgment and callback at 9:05 AM.
🚫 Instant DND / Opt-Out
If a customer says "Don't call" or asks not to be contacted, their number is immediately flagged in Sheetal CRM to block future automated calls.
🔒 Call Recording & Consent
Every call opens with a polite bilingual recording disclosure, and audio recordings are stored securely with full privacy protection for audit.
